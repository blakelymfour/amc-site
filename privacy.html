Adaptive Metabolic Controller

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 October 2026

Adaptive Metabolic Controller (AMC) is a personal nutrition and training system. This policy covers the Oura data AMC reads and this website.

Data AMC reads

AMC connects to an Oura account with OAuth and reads sleep and readiness data:

  • sleep duration, efficiency and stages
  • heart-rate variability
  • resting heart rate
  • temperature deviation
  • the readiness score

Storage and sharing

The data and the access tokens are stored in a private database. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

This website

This website sets no cookies and runs no analytics. Its host, GitHub Pages, may keep standard server logs.

Revoking access

You can revoke AMC’s access at any time from your Oura account. AMC can also delete its stored tokens.
